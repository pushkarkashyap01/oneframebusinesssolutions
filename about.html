About One Frame Business Solutions

The right people make a meaningful difference. We work to bring organisations and professionals together through considered recruitment, careful candidate evaluation and clear communication.

Who We Are

Recruitment built around people and business needs.

One Frame Business Solutions is an HR and recruitment solutions company based in New Delhi, India. We work with organisations to understand their hiring requirements and connect them with professionals whose skills and experience match the role.

We see recruitment as more than collecting resumes. It calls for an understanding of the position, thoughtful review of candidate profiles and communication that respects the expectations of both the employer and the candidate.

From identifying potential candidates to supporting interview coordination, our focus is to make each stage of the hiring process clear, organised and responsive.

Understanding Organisations

We take time to understand a business, its role requirements and the experience needed for a suitable appointment.

Reviewing Candidate Profiles

We review resumes and professional backgrounds to assess how a candidate's experience aligns with the opportunity.

Connecting the Right Fit

We work to connect suitable candidates with roles that reflect the needs of the organisation and the individual's professional background.

Supporting the Process

We help coordinate communication and interviews so that employers and candidates can move through the process with clarity.

What We Do Best

Recruitment and workforce support

Recruitment and Talent Acquisition

We help organisations identify and connect with professionals whose experience and capabilities align with their hiring needs.

CV Review and Candidate Screening

Interview Coordination

Our Philosophy

People build businesses

Good hiring starts with taking the time to understand people.

Recruitment decisions affect both an organisation's work and a candidate's career. We believe the process should therefore be approached with care, honesty and a clear understanding of what each side needs.

  • Understand the role before recommending candidates.
  • Consider professional experience in the context of the opportunity.
  • Keep communication clear with clients and candidates.
  • Be realistic about requirements, suitability and process expectations.

Our Commitment

Accountability at every stage

We want clients and candidates to know where they stand throughout the recruitment process. That means listening carefully to the requirement, communicating clearly and treating each interaction with professionalism.

Our aim is not simply to complete a hiring activity, but to support a considered connection between an organisation and the person who may become part of it.
