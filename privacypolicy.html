Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

One Frame Business Solutions ("One Frame", "we", "our" or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website, contact us or express interest in our recruitment services, and how that information may be used.

By using this website or submitting information through our contact or newsletter forms, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with our website and services.

Information you provide directly may include:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Your email address and telephone number, where provided.
  • The subject and contents of enquiries or messages you send us.
  • Information you voluntarily provide about your recruitment requirements, professional experience or career interests.
  • Your email address if you subscribe to our newsletter.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information or identity documents through general website forms unless we specifically request them through an appropriate channel.

Technical information:

When you visit the website, certain technical information may be processed by our hosting or website infrastructure. This may include your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, access times and diagnostic information, depending on the services and configurations in use.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we receive to:

  • Respond to enquiries and messages.
  • Understand and discuss recruitment and staffing requirements.
  • Communicate with candidates about relevant opportunities.
  • Provide information about our services.
  • Send newsletter communications where you have subscribed.
  • Maintain website security and address technical issues.
  • Improve our website, communication and service experience.
  • Meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

3. Recruitment and Candidate Information

If you contact us regarding employment opportunities or recruitment services, we may use the information you provide to understand your professional background, experience, skills and interests.

Where relevant to a recruitment opportunity, candidate information may be shared with a prospective employer or client for consideration. We will seek appropriate consent before sharing identifiable candidate information for this purpose, where required by applicable law.

Submitting an enquiry or expressing interest in a position does not guarantee an interview, placement or employment.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information as a business activity. We may share information in limited circumstances, including:

  • Service providers: With providers that support our website hosting, business email, communications and technical operations.
  • Recruitment clients: With prospective employers when relevant to a recruitment opportunity and subject to appropriate consent or another lawful basis.
  • Legal requirements: Where disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation, court order or lawful government request.
  • Business protection: Where reasonably necessary to protect our rights, website, operations or users.

5. Email and Website Service Providers

Our website is hosted using Vercel infrastructure. We use business email services to receive and respond to enquiries submitted through our website.

These providers may process information on our behalf as part of delivering their services. Their processing may be subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, managing recruitment communications, maintaining business records and meeting applicable legal obligations.

The appropriate retention period may vary depending on the nature of the information and the circumstances in which it was provided.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies where needed for website functionality, security, performance or other configured services.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function as intended if certain cookies are disabled.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information we hold about you. You may also be able to withdraw consent where processing is based on your consent.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

10. External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of those external websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before sharing information.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website and recruitment services are intended for businesses, professionals and individuals seeking employment opportunities. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through this website.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, website practices or applicable requirements. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
